Vegetable Fried Rice

A quick and tasty fried rice made with fresh vegetables and simple ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • Mixed vegetables
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

Heat oil in a pan. Add vegetables and cook for a few minutes. Add cooked rice and soy sauce. Mix everything well and cook for another 3–4 minutes.

Easy Creamy Pasta

A creamy and delicious pasta recipe that is perfect for a quick dinner.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 1 cup cream
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

Cook the pasta according to the package instructions. Sauté garlic in a pan, add cream and cheese, then mix with the cooked pasta.

Homemade Pancakes

Soft and fluffy pancakes that are perfect for breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Instructions

Mix all ingredients in a bowl. Heat a pan and pour a small amount of batter. Cook both sides until golden brown and serve warm.
